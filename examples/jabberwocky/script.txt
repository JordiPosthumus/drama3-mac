[hushed, conspiratorial storyteller, almost a whisper] Twas brillig, and the slithy toves did gyre and gimble in the wabe. All mimsy were the borogoves, and the mome raths outgrabe.

[suddenly urgent, warning] <whisper>Beware the Jabberwock, my son!</whisper> The jaws that bite, the claws that catch! [stern, trembling] Beware the Jubjub bird, and shun the frumious Bandersnatch!

[proud, resolute, gathering courage] He took his vorpal sword in hand. Long time the manxome foe he sought, so rested he by the Tumtum tree, and stood awhile in thought.

[hushed dread, building slowly] And as in uffish thought he stood, the Jabberwock, with eyes of flame, came whiffling through the tulgey wood, and burbled as it came!

[shrieking terror] ONE, TWO! ONE, TWO! AND THROUGH AND THROUGH! The vorpal blade went snicker-snack! [exhausted, triumphant] He left it dead, and with its head he went galumphing back.

[bursting with joy, shouting with glee] And hast thou slain the Jabberwock? Come to my arms, my beamish boy! [gleeful laugh] O frabjous day! CALLOOH! CALLAY! [chortling] He chortled in his joy. [hushed wonder, soft fade] Twas brillig, and the slithy toves did gyre and gimble in the wabe.
